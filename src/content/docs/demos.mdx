## Outline part of a photo with Segment Anything

Click a region to trace its outline. It runs on your device with WebGPU and finds
outlines only: you still name each part yourself.

## Estimate depth with Depth Anything V2

Shows which parts of a photo are closer to the camera, as relative depth, not
centimetres. The image goes to a Hugging Face server, so use a test image.

<SpaceEmbed
  figure="Fig. 3"
  title="Depth Anything V2"
  href="https://huggingface.co/spaces/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2"
  src="https://depth-anything-depth-anything-v2.hf.space"
/>

Worked example: [computer vision for products at end of life](/applications-map/cv-taxonomy/).

<details id="two-ways-to-add-a-demo">
<summary>Add a demo</summary>

- **Hosted app.** To add a Hugging Face Space, use
  [`SpaceEmbed`](repo:blob/main/src/components/SpaceEmbed.astro). It loads when the
  reader asks. For another host, embed the app with an `<iframe>` and include a link
  in case it can't load.
- **In-browser app.** Use [`ZeroShotDemo.tsx`](repo:blob/main/src/components/ZeroShotDemo.tsx)
  as an example. Add `<YourDemo client:visible />` to the `.mdx` page.

Set the `demo:` field in the matching
[application entry](repo:blob/main/src/data/applications.yaml) so readers can open
the demo from the [map](/applications-map/).

</details>
