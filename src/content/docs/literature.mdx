IE field and AI technique), **research practice** (reproducibility, verification,
disclosure), and the **ML landmarks** behind today's tools. Every entry says in one
line why it's worth your time.

## Community and governance

Where to meet the people doing this work, and where AI-in-IE sits in the wider
AI-for-climate effort:

<Resources kind="community" />

Three pages and reports from that wider effort:

- [Climate Change AI wiki: Industry](https://wiki.climatechange.ai/wiki/Industry): the community wiki's page on ML for industrial processes, supply chains and materials. There is no industrial ecology page yet.
- [Climate change and AI: recommendations for government action](https://oecd.ai/en/wonk/documents/climate-change-and-ai-recommendations-for-government-action) (GPAI, 2021): what governments can do to support AI for climate action, and to limit the climate risks of AI itself.
- [Grand challenge initiatives in AI for climate and nature](https://ccai-reports.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/grand-challenge-initiatives-ai-climate-nature.pdf) (Climate Change AI for the Bezos Earth Fund, 2024): lessons from 200+ challenges and open-innovation programmes on how to set up benchmarks and evaluation rules.
