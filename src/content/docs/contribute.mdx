This site is my proposal to the AI4IE working group. The group hasn't adopted it, and
nothing here speaks for the group. [Who runs the site](#who-runs-the-site) says who
decides and what the upkeep involves. Here is what I'd like from you:

- [Send](/contribute/?type=ai-mistake#send-a-contribution) one AI mistake you have seen in IE
  work. You can send it anonymously.
- [Send](/contribute/?type=golden-question#send-a-contribution) a question with a known answer,
  to test AI tools on. A question from your own subfield is best.
- Say what the site would need before the group could adopt it, or offer to help maintain it,
  on [Discord](https://discord.gg/zhHAZakXHX) or through the
  [form](/contribute/?type=other#send-a-contribution).
- [Send](/contribute/?type=resource#send-a-contribution) one paper, tool or dataset you use.
- [Vote](#which-checks-should-be-ie-norms) on which checks you'd support as IE norms (a straw
  poll).
- Join the group's [Discord](https://discord.gg/zhHAZakXHX) and fill in its
  [community survey](https://forms.gle/nGH54ZxJefY6ksGM6) (a Google Form).

## Which checks should be IE norms?

Vote for the checks you'd like to see as routine practice in AI-assisted industrial ecology
work. Pick as many as you want. This is a straw poll to inform the group's discussion, not a
group decision. It keeps only a total per check: [Privacy](/privacy/) has the details. The
checks themselves are on [Verify AI-assisted work](/best-practices/verify/).

## Send a contribution

You don't need a GitHub account. Your submission becomes a public issue on GitHub, and I
review it before anything goes on the site.

- **Paper, tool or dataset:** a link and a short note on why it belongs here.
- **Question with a known answer:** an IE question whose answer or check you know.
- **AI mistake seen in IE work:** what went wrong and how you caught it.
- **Other:** a correction, a missing topic, or your view on an [open question](/open-questions/).

## Who runs the site

Only I have write access to the repository and to the Cloudflare account behind the domain,
and I decide what goes in until the group adopts the site. Upkeep means checking each
submission against its source, reviewing the monthly dependency updates, fixing what the weekly
link check finds, sorting up to 25 new paper leads a week, and rechecking tool entries older
than a year. I'm looking for a second maintainer from the group, and the repository could move
to a working-group organisation. [GOVERNANCE.md](repo:blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md) and
[MAINTAINING.md](repo:blob/main/MAINTAINING.md) have the details.

## Other ways to take part

- **GitHub issues:** [open an issue](repo:issues/new/choose) to get notified when it's reviewed.
- **Pull requests:** edit the YAML data files or pages. [CONTRIBUTING.md](repo:blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
  has the schema and style rules.
- **Discord:** discuss it in the working group's [Discord](https://discord.gg/zhHAZakXHX).

## Related communities and reports
