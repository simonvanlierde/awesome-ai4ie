This site is my proposal to the AI4IE working group. The group hasn't adopted it, and
nothing here speaks for the group. [Governance](repo:blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md) says what
adoption would mean and who decides, and [Maintaining](repo:blob/main/MAINTAINING.md) what
the upkeep involves. Here is what I'd like from you:

- [Vote](#which-checks-should-be-ie-norms) on which checks you'd support as IE norms.
- [Send](/contribute/?type=golden-question#send-a-contribution) a candidate golden question for your
  subfield: an IE question with a known answer, for testing AI tools.
- [Send](/contribute/?type=resource#send-a-contribution) one paper, tool or dataset you use.
- [Send](/contribute/?type=ai-mistake#send-a-contribution) one AI mistake you have seen in IE work.
- Fill in the group's [community survey](https://forms.gle/nGH54ZxJefY6ksGM6) (a Google Form).
- Tell me what would stop the group adopting the site, on [Discord](https://discord.gg/zhHAZakXHX)
  or through the [form](/contribute/?type=other#send-a-contribution).

## Which checks should be IE norms?

Vote for the checks you'd like to see as routine practice in AI-assisted industrial ecology
work. Pick as many as you want. This is a straw poll to inform the group's discussion, not a
group decision. It keeps only a total per check: [Privacy](/privacy/) has the details. The
checks themselves are on [Verify AI-assisted work](/best-practices/verify/).

## Send a contribution

You don't need a GitHub account. Your submission becomes a public issue on GitHub, and I
review it before anything goes on the site.

- **Paper, tool or dataset:** a link and a short note on why it belongs here.
- **Golden question:** an IE question with a known answer or check.
- **AI mistake seen in IE work:** what went wrong and how you caught it.
- **Other:** a correction, a missing topic, or your view on an [open question](/open-questions/).

## Other ways to take part

- **GitHub issues:** [open an issue](repo:issues/new/choose) to get notified when it's reviewed.
- **Pull requests:** edit the YAML data files or pages. [CONTRIBUTING.md](repo:blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
  has the schema and style rules.
- **Discord:** discuss it in the working group's [Discord](https://discord.gg/zhHAZakXHX).

## Related communities and reports

<Resources kind="community" />

- [Climate Change AI wiki: Industry](https://wiki.climatechange.ai/wiki/Industry): the community wiki's page on ML for industrial processes, supply chains and materials. There is no industrial ecology page yet.
- [Climate change and AI: recommendations for government action](https://oecd.ai/en/wonk/documents/climate-change-and-ai-recommendations-for-government-action) (GPAI, 2021): what governments can do to support AI for climate action, and to limit the climate risks of AI itself.
- [Grand challenge initiatives in AI for climate and nature](https://ccai-reports.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/grand-challenge-initiatives-ai-climate-nature.pdf) (Climate Change AI for the Bezos Earth Fund, 2024): lessons from 200+ challenges and open-innovation programmes on how to set up benchmarks and evaluation rules.
