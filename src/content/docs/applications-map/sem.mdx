Socio-economic metabolism studies the materials a society takes in, stores and releases
([Pauliuk & Hertwich, 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolecon.2015.08.012)). The
applications below map building and infrastructure stocks from imagery and estimate
material flows.

Researchers mapped building and infrastructure stocks in Austria and Germany at 10 m
<Cite id="haberlHighResolutionMaps2021" />. A separate study mapped building stocks
globally at 90 m and estimated 547 Gt in 2019 (391–672 Gt). Independent local estimates
ranged from 53% to 200% of the corresponding mapped values
<Cite id="haberlGlobalBuiltEnvironment2024" />.
For material flows, Bayesian models combine expert estimates with reported data.
One study estimated 146 flows and stock changes for England's construction aggregates from
reported data covering 20% of the system plus expert priors <Cite id="masonBayesianMaterialFlow2025" />.
