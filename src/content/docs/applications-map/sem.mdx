Socio-economic metabolism studies the materials a society takes in, stores and releases.
Its applications here use images to map stocks in buildings and infrastructure, and
Bayesian models to estimate material flows
([Pauliuk & Hertwich, 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolecon.2015.08.012)).
