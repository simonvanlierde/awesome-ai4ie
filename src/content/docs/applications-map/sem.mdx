Socio-economic metabolism (SEM) studies the biophysical basis of a society: the materials it
takes in, keeps as stocks and lets go. It is the umbrella for material flow analysis (MFA)
and the stock models built on it
([Pauliuk & Hertwich, 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolecon.2015.08.012)). AI helps most
with the stocks, the materials built into buildings and infrastructure: it estimates them
from satellite, night-light and street-level images. Bayesian methods estimate flows that no
one measured. The [map](/applications-map/#socio-economic-metabolism) sums up what works today.
