Socio-economic metabolism studies the materials a society takes in, stores and releases
([Pauliuk & Hertwich, 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolecon.2015.08.012)). The
applications below map building and infrastructure stocks from imagery and estimate
material flows.

Researchers mapped building and infrastructure stocks in Austria and Germany at 10 m
resolution <Cite id="haberlHighResolutionMaps2021" />. A global map at 90 m resolution put
building stocks at 547 Gt in 2019 (391–672 Gt) <Cite id="haberlGlobalBuiltEnvironment2024" />.

For material flows, Bayesian models start from expert estimates and update them with
reported data. One study estimated 146 flows and stock changes for England's construction
aggregates. Reported data covered 20% of the system, and expert estimates filled the rest
<Cite id="masonBayesianMaterialFlow2025" />.
