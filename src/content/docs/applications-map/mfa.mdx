Material flow analysis (MFA) tracks how much of a material enters a system, such as a city
or a country, how much stays there and how much leaves. What stays is the stock: the
materials built into buildings and infrastructure. AI helps most with the stocks: it
estimates them from satellite, night-light and street-level images, and Bayesian methods
estimate flows that no one measured. The [map](/applications-map/#mfa--stocks-and-flows)
sums up what works today.
