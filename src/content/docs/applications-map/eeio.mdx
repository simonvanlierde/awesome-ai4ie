Environmentally extended input-output (EEIO) analysis links the trade between the sectors
of an economy to the emissions and resources behind it, which gives economy-wide
footprints. Multiregional tables (MRIO), such as EXIOBASE, do the same across countries.
The AI work here is thin so far: the entries fill gaps in input-output tables and turn them
into ML-ready emission factors. The [map](/applications-map/#input-output) has no summary
of this field yet.
