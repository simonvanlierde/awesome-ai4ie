Life cycle assessment (LCA) adds up the environmental impacts of a product or service over
its whole life, from raw materials to waste. A footprint is the same count for one
indicator, such as greenhouse gases. AI helps most with the data: it screens impacts
quickly, fills gaps in inventories, and pulls inventory values out of papers and reports.
The [map](/applications-map/#lca--footprinting) sums up what works today.
