Life cycle assessment (LCA) estimates environmental impacts from raw materials to
disposal. The applications below use AI to screen impacts, estimate missing inventory
values and extract data from papers.

In two case studies, a large language model retrieved data from papers with F1 scores of
0.82 for methanol and 0.86 for plastic packaging at end-of-life. F1 combines how many
answers were right with how many expected answers the model found
<Cite id="kumarLargeLanguageModelbased2025" />. Check retrieved values against the papers.
In a benchmark of 11 general-purpose language models on 22 LCA tasks, experts found at
least one inaccurate or misleading statement in 37.2% of responses
<Cite id="donaldsonExpertGroundedBenchmark2025" />.
