Life cycle assessment (LCA) estimates environmental impacts from raw materials to
disposal. The applications below use AI to screen impacts, estimate missing inventory
values and extract data from papers.

In a benchmark of 11 general-purpose language models on 22 LCA tasks, experts found at
least one inaccurate or misleading statement in 37.2% of responses
<Cite id="donaldsonExpertGroundedBenchmark2025" />. See
[how reliable AI is today](/best-practices/#how-reliable-is-ai-today).

Each application carries a label for how far its evidence goes: *Early studies* (reviews,
one study or a prototype), *Tested in research studies* (several studies or a benchmark) or
*Applied to real data* (checked at scale against an independent reference). Terms used below:
LCI is life cycle inventory data; zero-shot means the model had no training for the task; MAPE
is mean absolute percentage error; a model hallucinates when it states something false, such as
a citation that does not exist.
