## Applications by field

### Life cycle assessment

Researchers use machine learning to screen impacts and estimate missing inventory values.
In two case studies, a large language model retrieved data from papers with F1 scores of
0.82 for methanol and 0.86 for plastic packaging at end-of-life. F1 combines how many
answers were right with how many expected answers the model found
<Cite id="kumarLargeLanguageModelbased2025" />. Check retrieved values against the papers.
In a separate benchmark of 11 general-purpose language models on 22 LCA tasks, experts
judged 37% of responses inaccurate or misleading
<Cite id="donaldsonExpertGroundedBenchmark2025" />.

<FieldApplications ie="lca" />

### Socio-economic metabolism

This field includes material flow analysis (MFA) and stock modelling
([Pauliuk & Hertwich, 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolecon.2015.08.012)).
Researchers mapped building and infrastructure stocks in Austria and Germany at 10 m
<Cite id="haberlHighResolutionMaps2021" />. A separate study mapped building stocks
globally at 90 m and estimated 547 Gt in 2019 (391–672 Gt). Independent local estimates
ranged from 53% to 200% of the corresponding mapped values
<Cite id="haberlGlobalBuiltEnvironment2024" />.
For material flows, Bayesian models combine expert estimates with reported data.
One study estimated 146 flows and stock changes for construction aggregates in England
<Cite id="masonBayesianMaterialFlow2025" />.

### Input-output

One study estimates values in next year's input-output tables with deep learning.
It needs that year's row and column totals <Cite id="zhaoUsingDeepLearning2022" />.
Another publishes emission factors
derived from EXIOBASE for use in machine learning <Cite id="guoGlobalEmissionFactor2026" />.
<SuggestPaper gap="Input-output" />

### Circular economy

Most mapped applications here use computer vision. One study estimated the composition of
shredded e-waste using images recorded on a pilot sorting line
<Cite id="vogelgesangAutomatedMaterialFlow2025" />. In a washing machine study, software
read model numbers in about 96% of usable repair photos and 40% of usable recycler photos
<Cite id="sterkensProductLabelIdentification2023" />. The
[computer vision worked example](/applications-map/cv-taxonomy/) gives the test conditions.

<FieldApplications ie="circular" />

### Industrial symbiosis

Researchers look for firms that could use another firm's waste. In one study, a
language model built a network of waste-to-resource links from papers and found 24 of
45 known exchanges in Italy's Taranto district
<Cite id="zhaoConstructionWastetoresourceKnowledge2025" />. Another model scored above
0.82 F1 on held-out ecoinvent data and 0.63–0.73 on 40 real exchanges
<Cite id="zhaoNovelMachineLearningbased2025" />.
