What AI already does in each industrial ecology (IE) field, how mature each application is,
and where nobody has tried yet. Start with your field below. The map is part of a proposal to
the AI4IE working group: [suggest a correction](/contribute/?type=other#send-a-contribution).

## Applications by field

Each field page says what works today, how far the evidence goes, and which papers it rests on.

- <span id="life-cycle-assessment"></span>[Life cycle assessment](/applications-map/lca/): screening impacts, estimating missing inventory values and extracting data from papers.
- <span id="socio-economic-metabolism"></span>[Socio-economic metabolism](/applications-map/sem/): mapping building stocks from imagery and estimating material flows.
- <span id="input-output"></span>[Input-output](/applications-map/eeio/): estimating table values and emission factors.
- <span id="circular-economy"></span>[Circular economy](/applications-map/circular/): computer vision for waste, products and components.
- <span id="industrial-symbiosis"></span>[Industrial symbiosis](/applications-map/symbiosis/): matching one firm's waste to another firm's inputs.

### AI techniques with no tag yet

These techniques have no tag in the [vocabulary](/vocabulary/) yet. Some papers already use them
under another tag.

- **Graph neural networks & network analysis.** Examples to add include supply-chain,
  input-output and material-flow networks.
  <SuggestPaper gap="Graph neural networks & network analysis" />
- **Surrogate models & emulators.** Fast models trained to mimic a slow simulation, so you can run many scenarios.
  <SuggestPaper gap="Surrogate models & emulators" />
- **Causal inference.** Estimating effects such as rebound or policy impacts.
  <SuggestPaper gap="Causal inference" />
- **Reinforcement learning.** Examples to add include disassembly sequencing and process control.
  <SuggestPaper gap="Reinforcement learning" />
- **Knowledge graphs & ontologies.** Connecting inventory terms and matching flows across datasets. One [symbiosis](/applications-map/symbiosis/) paper builds a waste-to-resource knowledge graph.
  <SuggestPaper gap="Knowledge graphs & ontologies" />
- **Recommender & matching systems.** Matching plants to waste streams or products to emission factors. The [symbiosis](/applications-map/symbiosis/) and [carbon footprinting](/applications-map/lca/) papers do this, tagged by their text methods.
  <SuggestPaper gap="Recommender & matching systems" />
- **Time-series & forecasting.** Forecasting waste generation, demand or stock growth from past data.
  <SuggestPaper gap="Time-series & forecasting" />
- **Federated & privacy-preserving learning.** Training across institutions without pooling their data.
  <SuggestPaper gap="Federated & privacy-preserving learning" />
- **Explainability.** Checking what drove a model's result.
  <SuggestPaper gap="Explainability" />
