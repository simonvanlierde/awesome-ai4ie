This field studies what happens to products and materials after use: collection, sorting,
reuse, repair and recycling. It is where most of the published AI work sits. Most of it is
computer vision, which recognises, counts and measures waste and components so that they
can be sorted or taken apart. The [map](/applications-map/#waste-eol--circularity) sums up
what works today, and the [CV taxonomy](/applications-map/cv-taxonomy/) goes into more depth.
