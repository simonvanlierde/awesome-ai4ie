Industrial symbiosis turns one firm's waste or by-product into another firm's input.
Researchers mine papers, patents and past exchange records to find candidate matches.

One model scored an F1 above 0.82 (0 to 1, higher is better) on test pairs built from
ecoinvent, and 0.63–0.73 on 40 real exchanges <Cite id="zhaoNovelMachineLearningbased2025" />.
