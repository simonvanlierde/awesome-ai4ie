In industrial symbiosis, one firm's waste or by-product becomes another firm's input, as in an
eco-industrial park. Finding those matches is in part a recommender problem. Most of the AI
work so far mines papers, patents and reported exchanges for possible matches. The
[map](/applications-map/#industrial-symbiosis) sums up what works today.
