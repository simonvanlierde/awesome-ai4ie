Most applications here use computer vision to identify, count or measure products and
materials after use. See the [computer vision taxonomy](/demos/cv-taxonomy/) for what
each method measures and whether it was tested on lab or field photos.

One study estimated the composition of shredded e-waste from images recorded on a pilot
sorting line. The sorter did not act on the results: the models ran on recorded images
afterwards <Cite id="vogelgesangAutomatedMaterialFlow2025" />.
