The circular economy keeps products and materials in use. After use they are collected,
sorted, reused, remanufactured or recycled into secondary resources. This is where most of
the published AI work sits, and most of it is computer vision: it recognises, counts and
measures waste and components so that they can be sorted or taken apart. The
[map](/applications-map/#circular-economy) sums up what works today, and the
[CV taxonomy](/applications-map/cv-taxonomy/) goes into more depth.
