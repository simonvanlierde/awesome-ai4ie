Every IE result carries uncertainty: from the data, from the model and from the assumptions.
Bayesian methods quantify it by combining what experts already believe with noisy data. The
most developed work applies them to material flow analysis. The
[map](/applications-map/#uncertainty--bayesian-methods) sums up what works today.
